Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Tap Try it without an account to start studying; your progress is saved on that device. To keep it on all your devices, open the Me tab and tap Sign in or create an account. You can use Apple (on iPhone and iPad), Google, Microsoft or an email address, and your progress comes with you.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Me tab, tap Account, then Delete account, and confirm. It removes your account and all your progress immediately and permanently.

I forgot my password

If you signed up with an email and password, email us from that address and we will help you reset it. If you use Apple, Google or Microsoft, sign in with that button instead; there is no separate Prochoro password.

I lost my streak

A streak counts days with at least one finished lesson, in your device's own timezone. Miss a full day and it resets.

My progress is not saving

If the Me tab says your progress is saved on this device, the app cannot reach your account right now. Your progress is safe and syncs when the connection comes back; tap Try syncing again to retry. If it persists, email us.

Can I change my course?

Yes. Tap the course name at the top of the screen and pick another subject. Each subject keeps its own progress.

A question looks wrong

Please tell us which course and which topic it appeared under. Questions are original practice material, and mistakes are ours to fix.
