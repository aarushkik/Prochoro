Common questions

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in with Apple (on iPhone), Google, Microsoft or an email address. Your progress is saved to your account and syncs across your devices. If you studied as a guest in an earlier version, sign in on that same device and your progress comes with you.

How do I delete my account?

Profile, scroll to the bottom, Delete account. It removes your account and all your progress immediately and permanently.

I lost my streak

A streak counts days with at least one finished session, in your device's own timezone. Miss a full day and it resets. Some companions can shield a missed day — see the Characters screen.

My progress is not saving

If Profile shows a "Not syncing" banner, the app cannot reach your account. Progress is still safe on the device and will sync when the connection comes back. If it persists, email us.

Can I change my course?

Not yet. Each account is tied to the course chosen at setup. Being able to add a second course is on the list.

A question looks wrong

Please tell us which course and which topic it appeared under. Questions are original practice material, and mistakes are ours to fix.
