Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Prochoro is a study app for AP® exams. This policy describes exactly what it stores, why, and how to get rid of it. It is short because the app collects very little.

An account is optional

You can study without an account: tap Try it without an account. Your progress is then kept only on your device, and nothing about it is sent to us. If you sign in later on that device, the progress is added to your account, unless your account already follows a different course. An account keeps your progress on all your devices.

What we collect

Your account. When you sign in we receive your email address, and — if you sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft — the display name on that account. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email, in which case we receive a private relay address from Apple instead. We never receive your password for those providers. If you sign up with an email and password instead, the password is stored hashed by our authentication provider and is not visible to us.

Your study progress. Everything the app shows you about your own learning:

  • Which AP course you are studying, and where you chose to start
  • XP, gems, your current and longest streak, and the date of your last lesson
  • Which levels on each course path you have cleared, and how many lessons you have finished
  • Per-topic accuracy — how many questions you have seen and answered correctly for each skill
  • Which hat your platypus is wearing

Your best height in the endless climb and your Light/Dark setting are kept only on your device. They are not sent to us. Without an account, all of the progress above stays on your device too.

What we do not collect

  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No tracking of your activity across other apps or websites
  • No payment information — the app is free and has no purchases

Why we collect it

Solely to run the app for you: to keep your place on each path across devices, to hold your streak, and to work out which topics to recommend you practise. Your per-topic accuracy decides which topics the Practice tab suggests first. None of it is used for anything else.

Where it is stored

Signed-in profiles sync with Supabase, which hosts our database and handles authentication. Access is restricted at the database level so other students cannot read or write your profile. Authorized service administrators may access data to operate the service. A local copy also supports offline study.

Sign-in with Apple, Google or Microsoft is handled by those providers, under Apple's, Google's and Microsoft's own privacy policies. We ask them only for your email address and basic profile.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your data. We use the providers named above for authentication and hosting. They process the information needed to provide these services, subject to their own policies.

Deleting your account

You can delete everything from inside the app

Open the Me tab, tap Account, then Delete account, and confirm. This permanently removes your account and all the progress listed above, from our database and from this device, and cannot be undone. If you signed in with Apple, the app asks you to confirm with Apple and also ends Prochoro’s access to your Apple ID. Provider backups and operational logs follow the provider’s retention practices. You do not need to email anyone.

If you cannot reach the app for any reason, email the address below and we will delete it for you.

Studying without an account? Open the Me tab and tap Erase progress on this device. We hold nothing to delete.

Deleting your account also erases any earlier guest progress saved on that device. Removing the app alone may not clear what it stored on the device, so use the in-app delete action. We cannot reach copies saved on your other devices while they are offline, so sign out on those devices as well.

Children

Prochoro is intended for high-school students aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects what we collect, say so in the app.
